colab-controller

A personal automation tool for managing Google Colab runtimes.

colab-controller is a self-hosted controller that allocates, inspects, bootstraps, and releases Google Colab runtimes for workloads authorized by the account owner. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts to third parties.

The application uses Google OAuth only to access the Colab runtime APIs requested by the account owner. It does not read Google Drive files, Gmail, contacts, or other Google account data.
